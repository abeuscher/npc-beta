5. **Policies:** `FormPolicy` + `FormSubmissionPolicy`, registered in core `AuthServiceProvider::$policies` — travel via `Gate::policy()` at provider `boot()` (the Memberships precedent), the two map lines dropped.

## Front-loaded decisions

1. **Block shape: one session, three phases** (carve → package + redraw → extract) — ratified at the open gate; the 396 single-session precedent. Fallback: stop honestly after carve + package if the carve surfaces beyond the survey.
2. **The observer detaches by inversion** (ratify at the gate): the `#[ObservedBy]` attribute comes OFF the core `FormSubmission` model, and the plugin provider registers `FormSubmission::observe(FormSubmissionObserver::class)` at `boot()` — the Memberships `Membership::observe()` precedent on a core model. The observer, the mailable, and the `FormNotificationDeliveryException` travel with it. (Contrast with the 394 portal mailables, which stayed core because their senders are the model's *framework hooks* — this sender is detachable, so it travels.) Forms-absent: no observer, no notifications — and no submissions to notify about (the route is gone).
3. **Both email handles migrate to the plugin registry** (ratify at the gate) — surface-7 donations mechanics, contributed at `register()`, defaults byte-identical to today's seeder copy. **This absorbs the standing `portal_form_collision` housekeeping-inbox item** (`[0.388.01]` — the handle has no `EmailTemplate::defaults()` core-array entry, seeder-only copy): the registry contribution becomes the single source of its defaults, dissolving the array/seeder disagreement for this handle. The item's `admin_invitation` half (array has it, seeder lacks it) is core-owned and stays in the inbox — split the item at close, don't silently drop it.
4. **`<x-public-form>` travels — the first plugin-owned Blade component** (ratify at the gate): its sole consumer is the traveling widget template. Mechanics: the session picks the cleanest registration from the provider's `boot()` (`Blade::anonymousComponentPath()` with the plugin's views directory, or an aliased component) — whichever lands, the widget template keeps rendering `<x-public-form :handle>` byte-identically and the contract records the new surface note. This is new contract territory — flag it in the 0.21.0 changelog.
5. **`FormSeeder` gates on route presence** (recommended): the `DatabaseSeeder` call gates on `Route::has('forms.submit')` (the 393 tier-seeder precedent) — a forms-absent composition seeds no form rows and touches no forms table. The seeder file itself stays core for now (the surface-6 posture: the demo/config-pack layer is Stage E).
6. **Shape:** `plugins/Forms/`, namespace `Plugins\Forms\`, provider `FormsServiceProvider`, handle `forms`, package `nonprofitcrm/forms`, repo `crm-plugin--forms`. The widget nests under `Widgets/WebForm/`; the component under the plugin's views; controller → `Plugins\Forms\Http\Controllers\`; the route to the plugin route file at `boot()`. Manifest entry appended last, `folder` → `path` → `vcs` in-session. P6 mechanics verbatim; the runbook end-to-end.
7. **The redraw is the fifth**, two tables, **two sequences** (the first with two — record the count in the contract's surface-5 note), the SET-NULL contact FK as plain behavior, the internal CASCADE FK traveling with its tables. Byte-faithful raw-DDL create-table migrations in FK order; both identity checks ((a) full composition byte-identical; (b) forms-absent boots + seeds + smokes clean — every public page 200, `/forms/{handle}` POST 405/404 from the catch-all, zero form tables). Twin fixtures gain their migrator paths **vendor-relative directly** (the 393/395/396 compression nuance).
8. **Tests:** rule-5 pass into a new `Forms` phpunit testsuite; twins `FormsRemovedTestCase` + `FormsDisabledViaFlagTestCase` (with migrator paths — this plugin owns schema, unlike Blog); boundary guard `FormsModuleBoundaryTest` (zero `Plugins\Forms\` in `app/`, zero allowlist, positive control); removal mirror proves the route vanishes (POST → 405 from the GET catch-all), the widget row drops on sync, the admin surface vanishes, form data kept, the QuickActions entry yields null, and the collision seam simply never fires.
9. **Contract doc at close: 0.20.0 → 0.21.0** — Package shape + External repo eighth consumer; surface 5 fifth redraw consumer (the two-sequence note); surface 7 amended (both handles to the registry; the "permanently" wording resolved); the plugin-owned Blade component note (decision 4's landing); changelog. Plan doc D7 ✅; the D8 kickoff survey (Products/store disposition — the § 6.8 open question resolves at D8's prompt draft) captured at close; 398 prompts drafted.
