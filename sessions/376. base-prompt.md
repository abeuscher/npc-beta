## Process rules for every session

Base-prompt process rules apply in full. Session-specific:

- **This is the owner's idea. Treat it that way.** Your job is to give it the most rigorous, good-faith engineering examination it has ever gotten — steelman first, then stress-test. Neither rubber-stamp ("great idea, here's a 40-session plan") nor smother ("here are 19 reasons monorepos are better"). The owner asked for feasibility *thought*, at bare minimum — deliver genuine thought.
- **Questions are not instructions, and this session is mostly questions.** Ask them one or two at a time, in plain prose, and wait (`feedback_ask_the_short_question`, `feedback_no_multiple_choice_questions`). Do not stack a four-part questionnaire between every exchange.
- **Ground every claim in the actual code.** "The widget system already does X" gets a file reference; "composer can do Y" gets verified against the real `composer.json`, not recalled from training. Where a feasibility claim can be checked in five minutes (a path-repo spike, a `widgets:sync` dry read), check it rather than asserting it.
- **No implementation.** The deliverable is the plan doc (+ roadmap reconciliation). The one sanctioned exception is a throwaway spike that settles a load-bearing feasibility question — torn down after, findings into the doc.
- **Write the doc as the discussion happens,** not as a memory-dump at the end — the owner reviews and corrects it live. It is the session's artifact and the next sessions' canon.
- **Do not touch:** application code (beyond a torn-down spike), the FM contract surface, the 375 guards.
