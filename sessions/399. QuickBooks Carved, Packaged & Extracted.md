4. **The `TransactionRecorded` inversion** (ratify): core `Transaction::recordStripe` replaces `SyncTransactionToQuickBooks::dispatch(...)` with a core event dispatch (name settled at session; `App\Events\` or alongside the payment events — it is not payments-specific); the plugin's listener dispatches the traveling job. 5. **The QuickBooks settings sections move to a plugin-owned settings page** (ratify): discovered via the socket's `pagesPath`, same nav group/sort neighborhood as FinanceSettingsPage; the core page keeps its Stripe/branding half. The activity-log lines and env-switch modals travel byte-identically.
6. **The in-panel `routesFile` axis gets its first shipping consumer:** `routes/admin/quickbooks.php` → the plugin's `AdminContribution` `routesFile`. Route parity 272/272 byte-identical is the bar (names, URIs, middleware; moved-action FQCNs the 381 exception).
